Statistics

Statistics/College Information

The Office of Analytics & Institutional Research coordinates the collection of statistics and information about and for the college. This section provides frequently requested information that may be of interest or could help trigger further questions. Definitions of the data points below are established by national sources, such as the Common Data Set (CDS) or Integrated Post-Secondary Education Data System (IPEDS). If definitions deviate from these sources, we label them accordingly.

Factbook

In the Summer of 2025, the AIR Office developed a new online compendium of information about the students, employees, and finances of the institution that will hopefully be able to help people better tell the Dickinson story.  This link should be accessible to all employees, and we hope it is of use or at least of interest.  Please let us know if you have comments, suggestions, or ideas of what should be added for future iterations.
