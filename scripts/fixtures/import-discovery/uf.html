Common Data Set

The Common Data Set, or CDS, is a primary source of information for college guidebooks as well as prospective students who wish to learn about educational opportunities at the University of Florida.

It is the result of an effort to standardize and streamline the process of providing annual institutional data to publishers of college guides and other information sources.

All Common Data Sets are in PDF format.
